OUR LEGACY

The school Dr. Jose P. Laurel founded.

Lyceum of the Philippines University prides itself on its long and rich tradition of academic excellence through the legacy of its founder, Dr. Jose P. Laurel. The only Philippine President to have served in all three branches of the government, Dr. Laurel was a successful lawyer, legislator, constitutionalist, jurist, writer, scholar, statesman, philosopher, and above all things, educator.

With his admiration for knowledge and his appreciation of classical thought, Dr. Laurel named the school Lyceum of the Philippines after Lykeios, the site in ancient Athens where the great philosopher Aristotle nurtured the minds of his protégés.

“Veritas et Fortitudo, Pro Deo et Patria”

The school's motto reflects Dr. Laurel's belief in the value of learning and character formation for God and country.

Since opening in June 2008, LPU Cavite has produced more than 12,000 graduates who are now occupying different positions in government, non-government organizations, and the international arena.

LPU Cavite maintains above-national passing rates in various licensure examinations and has produced eleven (11) topnotchers over the past sixteen (16) years.

UNIVERSITY PROFILE

This is who we are.

Lyceum of the Philippines University – Cavite brings together students and educators in a collaborative learning environment. Through academic excellence, research, and innovation, the university encourages students to contribute to society and take an active role in nation-building.

Our 16-hectare campus is located in Cavite, a province known as the “Land of the Brave,” with a rich history, landmarks, museums, and leisure facilities. Our faculty members are leaders in their fields, passionate about teaching, and committed to advancing knowledge. With an average class size of 40, we foster a collaborative learning environment and meaningful student-faculty relationships.

Our alumni continue to make meaningful contributions to society as leaders and professionals in hospitality, law, nursing, business, engineering, and fine arts. Their achievements reflect the diverse opportunities and experiences that shape the Lycean community. We invite you to explore your future with LPU Cavite, guided by truth and fortitude, for God and country.

Vision and Mission

Vision

An internationally accredited university by 2030, dedicated to innovation and excellence in the service of God and country.

Mission

The Lyceum of the Philippines University, espousing the ideals of Jose P. Laurel, is committed to the following missions:

  • Instruction: Provide industry-based knowledge and skills
  • Research: Undertake multi-disciplinary research
  • Community Engagement: Support community engagement and social responsibility

  • Sustainability: Produce sustainability advocates for global action

EDUCATIONAL PHILOSOPHY

Developing the whole person.

Inspired by the founding ideals of “Veritas et Fortitudo” (Truth and Fortitude) and “Pro Deo et Patria” (For God and Country) of former Philippine President Jose P. Laurel, Lyceum of the Philippines University Cavite promotes outcome-based education based on a mostly progressive philosophy. Through learning-centered and experiential approaches, enriched by industry partnerships and international linkages, students cultivate critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Lycean education also focuses on developing the whole person, helping people become values-driven, socially responsible, creative, collaborative, and skilled individuals and leaders who can make a meaningful contribution to national development and global sustainability.

EDUCATIONAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

Committed to quality and continuous improvement.

Lyceum of the Philippines University Cavite is dedicated to forming world-class professionals, lifelong learners, and integral leaders. Our commitment extends to satisfying all applicable requirements, fostering a culture of continual improvement, and effectively managing intellectual property. We achieve this through the provision of quality research, instruction, community extension, and support services. Our pursuit of institutional development is guided by innovative and excellent educational organization management systems.

OBJECTIVES

Educational Organization Objectives

In implementing the educational organization policy, Lyceum of the Philippines University Cavite shall:

  1. Proactively FOCUS ON LEARNERS AND OTHER BENEFICIARIES through the engagement of interpersonal skills and work productivity in multidisciplinary, multicultural, and diverse teams.
  2. Develop learners' VISIONARY LEADERSHIP to become innovative leaders with an inquisitive mind in looking for opportunities to reflect and apply new knowledge and skills in a positive, sustainable way through research and project studies.
  3. Enact ENGAGEMENT OF PEOPLE in educational processes through planning and implementation.
  4. Employ a PROCESS APPROACH to embody lifelong learning strategies, leadership characteristics, and practical skills for one's life and survival.
  5. Facilitate CONTINUAL IMPROVEMENT of quality education through a responsive and relevant feedback mechanism via local and international quality assurance programs.
  6. Show good decision-making skills through critical thinking by weighing situations clearly and scrutinizing concerns and challenges with a rational and EVIDENCE-BASED DECISION AND APPROACH.
  7. Foster RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT with university stakeholders.
  8. Implement SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY through the demonstration and application of values-driven principles, ethical decision-making, and sustainability.
  9. Provide educational ACCESSIBILITY AND EQUITY through various programs and activities.
  10. Carry out ETHICAL CONDUCT in educational programs and learning processes.
  11. Implement DATA SECURITY AND PROTECTION mechanisms through strict adherence to government and other regulatory bodies' policies, standards, and guidelines.

    12. As reviewed and recommended by the Integrated Management System Committee on June 6, 2023. Originally approved effective June 16, 2023. Amended, approved and effective October 19, 2023 by Roberto P. Laurel, President.

PRESIDENT'S MESSAGE

Own your future with oneLPU.

Since its founding in 1952, Lyceum of the Philippines University Manila has remained committed to shaping individuals who will contribute meaningfully to the progress of our nation.

Today, that mission comes to life across our campuses as oneLPU, spanning Manila, Batangas, Laguna, Cavite, and Davao, united by shared values, strengthened by collaboration, and driven by a collective vision for excellence. Through generations of leadership, from my grandfather, Jose P. Laurel, to my father, Sotero H. Laurel, to the present, we have continued to evolve, ensuring that our education remains relevant, responsive, and future ready.

At LPU Manila, we prepare our students not only for employment, but for leadership. We offer industry-aligned programs, real-world learning experiences, and global opportunities that equip our graduates with the skills, mindset, and character to thrive in a competitive and interconnected world. Here, students are empowered to discover their strengths, pursue their ambitions, and create meaningful impact.

Choosing LPU Manila means becoming part of a community that values excellence, integrity, and purpose, where your potential is recognized, your voice is heard, and your future is shaped with intention.

As part of oneLPU, we move forward together, honoring our legacy while building the future.

Learn Different. Live Different.
Own your future with oneLPU.
